About Rift Dive

Rift Dive is an unofficial, fan-made daily trivia game about League of Legends: champions, lore, music, pro play, skins and game knowledge.

How scoring works

Each prompt has many accepted answers. Every accepted answer carries a fixed familiarity score (how well known it is), which maps to one of six tiers worth 10, 15, 30, 60, 85 or 100 points. Obvious answers earn little; deep cuts earn a lot. Other players’ answers never change your score.

Open source

The engine is derived from the MIT-licensed OpenKrillion project (an independent open-source game), reskinned for League. Source on GitHub ↗

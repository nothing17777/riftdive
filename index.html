What Rift Dive is

Rift Dive is a daily League of Legends trivia game with one twist: the right answer is only the start. Each prompt, such as a champion from Demacia or a member of Pentakill, has many accepted answers, and the less obvious the one you choose, the deeper you dive. Everyone gets the same seven prompts each day. There are no multiple-choice hints, so your own League knowledge does the work.

How a dive works

Press Begin Descent. Each of the seven rounds starts with a three-second preview so you can read the prompt, then you get twenty-five seconds to type one answer. Submit before the clock runs out. A typo that is close to a known answer is offered as a correction, and you confirm it before it scores. Anything that does not fit the prompt scores zero.

The six rarity tiers

Every accepted answer belongs to a tier: Minion (10), Big Brain (15), Jungler (30), Elite (60), Elder (85) and Pentakill (100). Tiers come from a fixed familiarity score for each answer, meaning how widely known it is among League players. They are not based on what other players typed today, so your score cannot be changed by anyone else.

Depth and score

Every point sinks you ten metres. Seven Pentakill answers would total 700 points and 7,000 metres, the Rift Core. Most dives land somewhere in the middle, and a single great deep cut can matter more than three safe picks.

What the prompts cover

Champions by region and role, the lore of Runeterra, the Arcane series, summoner spells and items, neutral objectives, skins and skin lines, League music from Pentakill and K/DA to the Worlds anthems, and professional play, including Worlds winners and the major leagues.

Tips for rarer answers

Read the exact wording: a condition like a region or a role limits the field. Skip the first champion that comes to mind and think about who else fits. The first answer is usually everyone's first answer. Less-played champions, older lore and smaller regions are where Elder and Pentakill answers live.

Practice, history and streaks

You get one scored daily dive. Practice dives are unlimited but do not change your daily score or streak. Your logbook is saved in this browser and needs no account.

Quick questions

Is Rift Dive official?

No. Rift Dive is an unofficial, fan-made game. It isn’t endorsed by Riot Games and doesn’t reflect the views of Riot Games or anyone officially involved in producing or managing Riot Games properties. Riot Games and all associated properties are trademarks or registered trademarks of Riot Games, Inc.

How is an answer’s rarity decided?

Every accepted answer has a fixed familiarity score set by us: how widely known it is among League players. Famous answers earn few points; obscure but correct ones earn many. Other players’ answers do not change your score.

Do my scores sync across devices?

No account sync is offered. Your browser stores a local history copy; this service resumes dives using a random browser identifier.

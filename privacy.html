Privacy at Rift Dive

You can play without an account. A random browser identifier, not a name or email, resumes your dive. This page describes what the service actually stores.

Game progress

Preferences, draft answers and a copy of completed history are stored in your browser. The server stores your random identifier, the game date, accepted answers, scores and round timing. Clearing browser storage removes the local copy and identifier.

Statistics and feedback

Daily score comparisons use completed daily games on Rift Dive. Practice scores are excluded. Voluntary ratings and suggestions are saved if submitted; do not include personal information.

Analytics

No analytics are enabled by default.

Cookies

The essential ok_player cookie is HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax and Secure on HTTPS. It identifies a browser's game history.

Retention and requests

Game records are kept for history and statistics. Rate-limit records are short-lived. We do not sell player records and collect no payment information.
